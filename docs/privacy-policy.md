# Pillhaven privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Pillhaven is a free Android app for keeping track of your family's medicines and getting reminded to take them. It is made by Hemz, an independent developer. Questions about this policy: [hemzz.devv@gmail.com](mailto:hemzz.devv@gmail.com).

## The short version

Your medicines, schedules, dose history, photos and notes are kept on your phone. Pillhaven has no account, no analytics and no ads, and the developer can't see any of your data.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter into Pillhaven is stored in the app's private storage on your phone:

- medicines, their doses, forms and schedules
- which doses were taken, skipped or missed
- photos of packs and strips, and your notes
- your settings

None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy Pillhaven's data (medicines, dose history, photos and notes) to your own Google account, encrypted, so you can get it back on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google and you; the developer can't see it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings. Android limits how much an app can back up, so a backup may not include every photo if you have many.

## Looking up ingredients

To show what's in a medicine (its chemical formula and what it's used for), Pillhaven looks up the ingredient's name, for example "Paracetamol", online. Only the ingredient name is sent, over an encrypted connection (https), to these free public services:

- [RxNorm](https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/RxNav/) (US National Library of Medicine)
- [PubChem](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) (US National Institutes of Health)
- [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/) (US National Library of Medicine)

Nothing else is sent with it: no personal information, brand names, schedules or device identifiers. Like any website, these services can see your internet address when the app connects to them.

Searching the Indian brand list happens on your phone, offline.

## Reading medicine packs

When you scan a pack, the text is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit text recognition. Its usage statistics are switched off, and the photo never leaves your phone.

## Health Connect (optional meal reminders)

If you connect Health Connect, Pillhaven reads only the meal (nutrition) records you log in other apps, including in the background, so it can remind you to take medicines marked "after a meal" at the right time. Pillhaven never writes to Health Connect, and the meal data stays on your phone: it isn't sent anywhere, shared, sold or used for anything else. Pillhaven's use of information received from Health Connect follows the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134), including its Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time in Health Connect's settings.

## Ordering from pharmacies

The Order button opens Tata 1mg, PharmEasy or Netmeds in your browser, searching for the medicine's name. Pillhaven doesn't send them anything else. What you do on their sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

## Permissions

- **Notifications, alarms and full-screen alarms:** to remind you when a dose is due, even when the phone is locked. On the lock screen, notifications only say that a medicine is due; names appear after you unlock.
- **Camera (through your camera app):** to photograph packs, only when you choose to.
- **Internet:** for the ingredient lookups above.
- **Health Connect:** only if you connect it, as described above.

## Children

Pillhaven is meant for adults managing medicines. It isn't directed at children.

## Deleting your data

Delete a medicine in the app to remove it and its history. To remove everything, clear Pillhaven's storage in your phone's app settings, or uninstall it. Backups in your Google account can be deleted from your Google account settings.

## Medical advice

Pillhaven doesn't give medical advice. Ingredient information is general reference material. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
